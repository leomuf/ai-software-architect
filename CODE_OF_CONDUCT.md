# Code of Conduct

AI Software Architect welcomes constructive participation from people of different
backgrounds and experience levels.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and evidence;
- welcome reasonable questions and good-faith disagreement;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and unwanted
  disclosure of private information;
- respect security-reporting and confidentiality boundaries; and
- help keep discussions relevant and technically useful.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, off-topic, or privacy-violating
content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact channel on
[AUTOSOFT Engineering](https://www.autosoft-engineering.de). Do not include
credentials or confidential repository content.
